About InAmigos Foundation

More Than an NGO —
A Movement for Change

Founded by Mr. Govind Shukla, Founder & CEO

InAmigos Foundation was born on September 23, 2020, with a simple but powerful belief: that compassion, when organized, becomes transformation. Headquartered in Bilaspur, Chhattisgarh, we have grown into a nationwide force for social good.

We work across education, food distribution, women's empowerment, animal welfare, environmental sustainability, and skill development — driving measurable change in underserved communities across 28 Indian states through a team of over 28 dedicated volunteers and thousands of supporters.

Our Vision

A kinder, more equal society where no one is left behind.

Our Mission

Uplift underprivileged communities through systematic, programmatic action.

Transparency

80G & 12A certified — every rupee is accountable.

Collective Action

We believe impact multiplies when communities unite.

Flagship Initiatives

Six Projects,
One Purpose

Each initiative targets a critical gap in society — together they form a holistic ecosystem of change.

Project SEVA

Providing essential food and clothing to the homeless and underprivileged. Regular distribution drives ensure no one goes hungry or cold — a daily act of service rooted in selfless giving.

50,000+ Meals Distributed

Project BACHPANSHALA

Bridging educational gaps for underprivileged children through basic digital literacy, life skills training, and school curriculum support — because every child deserves a chance to learn.

100s of Children Impacted

Project JEEV

Dedicated to the welfare of stray and injured animals. Our volunteer network feeds 50+ stray animals daily, providing care, medical assistance, and advocacy for voiceless lives.

50+ Animals Fed Daily

Project UDAAN

Empowering rural women through self-help group collaborations, financial independence programs, skill development workshops, and menstrual hygiene awareness campaigns.

Women-Led Self-Help Groups

Project PRAKRITI

Advocating for environmental conservation through large-scale tree planting drives and eco-friendly farming support — building a greener, more sustainable tomorrow for future generations.

20,000+ Saplings Planted

Project VIKAS

Enabling career growth through internships and skill training in digital marketing, finance, content writing, social work, data operations, and more — creating job-ready changemakers.

30,000+ Interns Trained
